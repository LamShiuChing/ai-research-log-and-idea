# LM optimization: how small GPTs (5M–85M) learn reasoning — 31 findings
**Dates:** 2026-09-13 to 2026-09-16 · **Status:** Ongoing (31 findings recorded; further runs listed as planned in the docs) · **Hardware/scale:** Local RTX 4080 SUPER 16 GB, i9-14900KF, 32 GB RAM; later runs on rented RTX 5090 instances (Vast.ai). Models of 5M–88M parameters, trained for 100M–400M tokens per run · **Code:** https://github.com/LamShiuChing/LM-optimization

## Question
Can small GPT-style models trained from scratch (no pretrained weights) learn multi-step procedures, and what decides whether they keep working on longer inputs and on unseen combinations of procedures? The study varies one thing at a time: the written form of the reasoning, the tokenizer unit, the position scheme, the data mix and the held-out combinations.

## Approach
- **Models:** GPT-2-style decoders trained from scratch at four sizes (5m, 14m, 38m, 85m). Parameter counts differ across findings because the vocabulary changed: 4.9M / 14.5M / 38.2M / 85.6M with the byte vocabulary (F1–F2), 5.8M / 15.8M / 39.9M / 88.2M with BPE-4096 (F12).
- **Tokenizers:** byte-level (256 symbols) at first (F1–F6); own byte-pair encoding at 1024, 4096 and 512 for task traces (F7); from F10 on, a BPE-4096 vocabulary for prose with digits and operator symbols kept as single units ("symbol-preserving" codec, F11).
- **Data:** TinyStories prose as the text corpus (F1, F7, F10 onward). Synthetic reasoning tasks written as text traces: addition, multiplication, parity, reachability, compare, running max, count, bracket matching, two- and three-stage compositions, nested calls, a file-system agent, English-phrased requests, generated chat dialogues (F30, from Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct-AWQ), and a calculator plus fact store in the loop (F31).
- **Positions:** learned absolute, none, and rotary compared (F4). From F13 on, no positional encoding with a per-document attention mask.
- **Training:** AdamW with warmup and cosine schedule. Batch 64x512 for the size ladder and stream runs, 128 for single-task runs. Learning rate 6e-4 for the ladder and stream runs; 1e-3 for the first single-task runs (F3). No learning-rate sweep for most runs.
- **Evaluation:** exact-match accuracy on fresh problems at the training lengths and at lengths beyond them (out-of-distribution, "OOD"). Prose is scored in bits per byte on TinyStories validation text. Most findings are single runs; where seeds were run, the docs report mean and standard deviation.

## What it showed
- **Length generalisation depends on the text and on the positions.** Rotary positions gave zero extrapolation to 6-digit addition at every size from 5.8M to 88.2M (F4, F12). A model with no positions and a per-document attention mask reached 0.74 at 6 digits in a mixed prose stream (F13), though 7 digits stayed at 0.00. The no-position result is one run.
- **How numbers are written matters more than model size.** Through a tokenizer's chunks, 5-digit addition was 0.04–0.09 (F10). As single symbols it was 0.81–0.99 (F10), and the corrected step-format runs gave 0.83–0.95 (F11). The step format, which writes the operands each step consumes, extrapolated better than the compact formats (F11).
- **Unseen combinations fail when each stage has few partners, and succeed when it has many.** English composition went from 0.08 with seven trained pairs (F27) to 0.81 with 34 trained pairs (F29). Inside the internal language, the same pattern held: naming the held-out stage correctly rose from 0 of 40 (F17) to 30 of 30 on three of four held-out pairs (F19). Two of four pairs were solved in both seeds, so the result is only partly stable.
- **Tools work on trained structures and do not compose on new ones.** A 5M model with a calculator and fact store solved all 200 fresh problems of trained structures and refused all 22 unknown keys, but got 0.08 on held-out structures (F31).
- **Several early headlines were revised.** The 0.81 addition result in F10 was the best of five runs and was superseded by F11. The agent column in F26 was an evaluator error and was corrected in F27. Evaluator defects in F30 and F31 were found by printing what the environment received and then fixed.

## Limitations
- Most findings are single runs (one seed). Some differences are within seed spread, such as addition extrapolation in F8 (0.86, 0.37, 0.80 across seeds) and reachability selection in F19. The plan in `docs/research-plan.md` asks for at least three seeds per reported number; many findings do not meet that.
- No learning-rate sweep for most runs, and no size-scaled token budget (F2, F12). The size ladder is a smoke test, not a scaling law.
- TinyStories prose and synthetic tasks with tiny vocabularies. The English requests are templates and generator-written phrasings, not human-written English (F26). Held-out phrasings and held-out pairs were chosen by hand in several findings.
- Chat dialogues were generated by Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct-AWQ and filtered; held-out chat measures share the generator's style (F30). Multi-turn sessions across registers were not tested (F31; `docs/progress.md`).
- Continuous-latent and looped-model experiments used one recipe with no curriculum and no learning-rate sweep (F6).
- Parameter counts changed with the vocabulary (4.9M vs 5.8M for the 5m model), so sizes are not identical across findings.
- The F22 headline gives a trained-length range of 0.95–1.00 for the held-out chains, but the table's first column includes 0.89 (runmax.inc.find). This log uses the table values.
- Compute and hardware differed between runs (local 4080 SUPER, rented 5090s, `torch.compile` in some runs), which affects timing comparisons but not the accuracy numbers.

## Key files
Paths relative to the repository root.
- `src/data.py`: downloads TinyStories and writes byte-level training and validation streams.
- `src/bpe.py`: own byte-pair encoding, with an option that keeps digits and symbols as single tokens.
- `src/model.py`: GPT-2-style decoder with learned or no positional encoding and the per-document attention mask (F13).
- `src/train.py`: stream training with reasoning documents mixed into prose; flags for dataset version, held-out pairs, the shared operator codec and chat data.
- `src/train_task.py`: trains and evaluates one synthetic task in and out of distribution (addition, multiplication, parity, reachability and others).
- `src/tasks.py`: synthetic task generators, dataset versions v1–v20 and the nested and chained forms.
- `src/formats.py`: alternative trace formats for the same reasoning (F8).
- `src/harness.py`: exact-match accuracy, reasoning bytes and FLOPs per problem.
- `src/latent.py`: continuous latent thoughts, Coconut-style (F6).
- `src/english.py`, `src/en_probe.py`: English request templates and the translation probe (F26–F29).
- `src/agent.py`, `src/agent_probe.py`: file-system agent loop and episode classification (F24–F25).
- `src/chat.py`, `src/chat_gen.py`, `src/chat_filter.py`, `src/chat_eval.py`: chat corpus plan, generation, filtering and measures (F30).
- `src/computer.py`, `src/problems.py`: calculator and fact store, and the word problems for it (F31).
- `src/stage_names.py`, `src/copy_probe.py`: stage-name and operand-copy probes (F19, F21, F23).
- `src/ablation.py`, `src/summarize.py`: aggregate runs across seeds and collect results into a table.
- `docs/findings.md`: the 31 findings with setups, numbers and caveats.
- `docs/dataset.md`, `docs/language.md`, `docs/agent.md`, `docs/english.md`, `docs/chat.md`, `docs/computer.md`: design notes for each domain.
